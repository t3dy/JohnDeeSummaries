Introduction

Introduction - Harkness. key terms: Book of Nature; natural philosophy; angel diaries; historiography; apocalypse. Harkness frames Dee's angel conversations as a continuation of his natural philosophy rather than a late-career aberration.

The Colloquium of Angels: Prague, 1586

The Colloquium of Angels: Prague, 1586 - Harkness. key terms: Prague; Budovec; Kelley; scryers; showstone; holy table; Liber Logaeth; Rudolf II; Pucci. Uses Prague to show Dee as public prophet, communicator, natural philosopher, and political-religious actor.

Building Jacob's Ladder: The Genesis of the Angel Conversations

Building Jacob's Ladder: The Genesis of the Angel Conversations - Harkness. key terms: Jacob's ladder; Scripture; Propaedeumata; Monas; mathematics; optics; cabala. Explains how Dee's earlier work prepared the angel conversations: Scripture and Nature as books, Propaedeumata as optical/celestial causation, Monas as symbolic decipherment, and mathematics as mediator.

Climbing Jacob's Ladder: Angelology as Natural Philosophy - Harkness. key terms: angelology; Pseudo-Dionysius; Agrippa; Trithemius; prayer; hierarchy; showstone. Shows why angels could function as natural-philosophical mediators.

The True Cabala: Reading the Book of Nature

The True Cabala: Reading the Book of Nature - Harkness. key terms: cabala; divine language; Adamic language; Book of Soyga; Liber Logaeth; true names; Enoch. Argues that the angels revealed a true cabala of nature grounded in divine/Adamic language, true names, tables, gates, airs, governors, and Enochic material.

Adam's Alchemy: The Medicine of God and the Restitution of Nature

Adam's Alchemy: The Medicine of God and the Restitution of Nature - Harkness. key terms: alchemy; medicine of God; Paracelsus; restitution; corruption; resurrection; Adam. Completes the program: after reading nature through cabala, Dee receives restored Adamic alchemy and the medicine of God to heal nature's corruption.

Epilogue

Epilogue - Harkness. key terms: Casaubon; reception; Ashmole; Hooke; Boyle; manuscripts; later reputation. Traces collapse of Dee and Kelley's partnership, Dee's later disappointment, manuscript survival, Casaubon's skeptical framing, and later antiquarian/natural-philosophical interest.
