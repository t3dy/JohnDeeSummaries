Orientation to the early actions

Orientation to the early actions is part of Primary Sections. 1581-1583 - Conversations with spirits. Editorial introductions frame Dee's spirit actions as a documentary corpus: devotional prayer, learned natural philosophy, scrying practice, political hope, and ritual experimentation. They establish the main people and tools: Dee, Edward Kelley/Talbot, earlier scryer Barnabas Saul, the shew-stone, holy table, wax seals, prayers, and the expectation that angelic instruction could recover lost wisdom. Source: John Dees Five Books of Myster Joseph H Peterson.pdf, Christopher Whitby John Dee s Actions with Spirits _ 22 December 1581 to 23 May 1583 Routledge.pdf.

Mysteriorum Liber Primus

Mysteriorum Liber Primus is part of Primary Sections. Dec. 22, 1581-Mar. 1582 - Conversations with spirits. The opening book begins with Dee's urgent prayer for divine wisdom and moves into the earliest surviving angelic sessions. The material shows Dee testing the legitimacy of what appears in the stone, asking whether the spirits are good, and seeking instruction that will serve God rather than curiosity or treasure-seeking. Barnabas Saul and then Edward Kelley/Talbot enter the record as scryers. Source: John Dee Mysteriorum Liber Primus.pdf, Primus.pdf, John Dee Primus.pdf.

Mysteriorum Liber Secundus

Mysteriorum Liber Secundus is part of Primary Sections. Mar. 1582 - Conversations with spirits. The second book deepens the apparatus of the angelic work. Liber Mysteriorum Quintus

Liber Mysteriorum Quintus is part of Primary Sections. Mar.-May 1583 - Conversations with spirits. The fifth book moves toward culmination of the first phase: fuller angelic hierarchies, more elaborate tables and names, and the sense that Dee's actions are part of a larger providential disclosure. The material connects spiritual instruction to future reform, hidden knowledge, and the proper use of revealed names. It also shows growing dependence on Kelley's visions and the strain of turning visionary events into stable textual records. A True & Faithful Relation: Casaubon preface and framing

A True & Faithful Relation: Casaubon preface and framing is part of Primary Sections. Published 1659; framing Dee records - Conversations with spirits. Casaubon frames Dee's diaries as a cautionary publication about spirits, credulity, demonic deception, and the risks of unlawful curiosity. His preface is not Dee's voice, but it strongly shapes the printed reception of the diaries. It argues over whether the events should be believed, how they relate to possession and revelation, and why publishing them might serve religious warning. A True & Faithful Relation: later angelic actions

A True & Faithful Relation: later angelic actions is part of Primary Sections. 1583-1587, plus later fragments - Conversations with spirits. The later actions extend the Dee-Kelley angelic work beyond the first Five Books into continental travel, increasingly elaborate revelations, the Enochian calls, tables, governors, and fraught commands. The records preserve both technical angelic material and the human drama of obedience, doubt, conflict, and dependence on Kelley's visions. They are essential for the mature Enochian system but must be read as posthumously edited printed diaries.
