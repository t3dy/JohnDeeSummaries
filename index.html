Orientation to the early actions

Orientation to the early actions is part of Primary Sections. 1581-1583 - Conversations with spirits. Editorial introductions frame Dee's spirit actions as a documentary corpus: devotional prayer, learned natural philosophy, scrying practice, political hope, and ritual experimentation. They establish the main people and tools: Dee, Edward Kelley/Talbot, earlier scryer Barnabas Saul, the shew-stone, holy table, wax seals, prayers, and the expectation that angelic instruction could recover lost wisdom. Source: John Dees Five Books of Myster Joseph H Peterson.pdf, Christopher Whitby John Dee s Actions with Spirits _ 22 December 1581 to 23 May 1583 Routledge.pdf.

Mysteriorum Liber Primus

Mysteriorum Liber Primus is part of Primary Sections. Dec. 22, 1581-Mar. 1582 - Conversations with spirits. The opening book begins with Dee's urgent prayer for divine wisdom and moves into the earliest surviving angelic sessions. The material shows Dee testing the legitimacy of what appears in the stone, asking whether the spirits are good, and seeking instruction that will serve God rather than curiosity or treasure-seeking. Barnabas Saul and then Edward Kelley/Talbot enter the record as scryers. Source: John Dee Mysteriorum Liber Primus.pdf, Primus.pdf, John Dee Primus.pdf.

Mysteriorum Liber Secundus

Mysteriorum Liber Secundus is part of Primary Sections. Mar. 1582 - Conversations with spirits. The second book deepens the apparatus of the angelic work. Dee receives instructions around the seal, holy names, and ritual furniture, with repeated attention to purity, obedience, and the danger of misunderstanding revealed forms. Michael's authority becomes central, and the conversations move from exploratory scrying into a more formal program of preparation. Source: John Dee Mysteriorum Liber Secundus.pdf, Secundus.pdf, John Dees Five Books of Myster Joseph H Peterson.pdf.

Mysteriorum Liber Tertius

Mysteriorum Liber Tertius is part of Primary Sections. Apr. 1582 - Conversations with spirits. The third book continues the establishment of angelic authority and ceremonial order. Dee presses for knowledge, protection, and clarity, while the spirits redirect him toward patience, humility, and the correct sequence of divine instruction. The sessions refine names, offices, and procedures rather than offering a single linear doctrine. Liber Mysteriorum Quintus

Liber Mysteriorum Quintus is part of Primary Sections. Mar.-May 1583 - Conversations with spirits. The fifth book moves toward culmination of the first phase: fuller angelic hierarchies, more elaborate tables and names, and the sense that Dee's actions are part of a larger providential disclosure. The material connects spiritual instruction to future reform, hidden knowledge, and the proper use of revealed names. It also shows growing dependence on Kelley's visions and the strain of turning visionary events into stable textual records. De Heptarchia Mystica: title, testimonies, and warrant

De Heptarchia Mystica: title, testimonies, and warrant is part of Primary Sections. Compiled from 1582-1583 actions - Ritual compilation. The opening of De Heptarchia Mystica explains the authority and purpose of the heptarchical system. Dee frames the material as divinely taught, grounded in the angelic actions, and ordered toward lawful communication with angelic governors. Editorial matter identifies it as a transcription from Dee's manuscript and emphasizes its relation to the larger diaries. Source: John Dee De Heptarchia Mystica.pdf.

De Heptarchia Mystica: kings, princes, ministers, and offices

De Heptarchia Mystica: kings, princes, ministers, and offices is part of Primary Sections. Planetary/heptarchical system - Ritual compilation. The central section organizes the sevenfold angelic hierarchy: kings, princes, and ministers with specific names, powers, and offices. It translates visionary instruction into a functional system for petitions, invocations, and requests for knowledge or assistance. Liber 84 and the Forty-Eight Keys/Calls

Liber 84 and the Forty-Eight Keys/Calls is part of Primary Sections. Later angelic corpus, especially 1584 material - Revealed texts and calls. Liber 84 preserves revealed textual/table material connected with the angelic or Enochian language, elemental tablets, seniors, calls, and names. It is less a narrative diary than a structured body of revealed language and magical geography. The section is important for the transition from the early angelic sessions to the later system of calls, watchtowers, governors, and tabular correspondences. Primary Sections · Conversations with spirits

A True & Faithful Relation: Casaubon preface and framing

A True & Faithful Relation: Casaubon preface and framing is part of Primary Sections. Published 1659; framing Dee records - Conversations with spirits. Casaubon frames Dee's diaries as a cautionary publication about spirits, credulity, demonic deception, and the risks of unlawful curiosity. His preface is not Dee's voice, but it strongly shapes the printed reception of the diaries. It argues over whether the events should be believed, how they relate to possession and revelation, and why publishing them might serve religious warning. A True & Faithful Relation: later angelic actions

A True & Faithful Relation: later angelic actions is part of Primary Sections. 1583-1587, plus later fragments - Conversations with spirits. The later actions extend the Dee-Kelley angelic work beyond the first Five Books into continental travel, increasingly elaborate revelations, the Enochian calls, tables, governors, and fraught commands. The records preserve both technical angelic material and the human drama of obedience, doubt, conflict, and dependence on Kelley's visions. They are essential for the mature Enochian system but must be read as posthumously edited printed diaries.
