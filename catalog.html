Monas Hieroglyphica / The Hieroglyphic Monad

Monas Hieroglyphica / The Hieroglyphic Monad is part of Works. John Dee, authored; collection contains modern English editions/translations and related scholarship Readable primary-text copies are present in three PDF variants; several article/review files discuss the Monas but are not Dee writings.. Dee's emblematic and numerological treatise presents the Monad symbol as a compressed key to cosmic, mathematical, astrological, cabalistic, and alchemical order. The work moves through short theorem-like propositions, unpacking the point, line, cross, lunar and solar signs, Aries, and elemental/celestial correspondences. In LLM terms, it is dense symbolic natural philosophy rather than narrative prose: useful chunks cluster around definitions of the symbol, mathematical analogies, planetary/alchemical correspondences, and claims that a single hieroglyph can reconcile multiple learned disciplines. Source: John Dee - The Hieroglyphic Monad-Weiser Books (2000).pdf, John Dee The Hieroglyphic Monad.pdf, The Hieroglyphic Monad.pdf.

De Heptarchia Mystica is part of Works. John Dee manuscript/compilation; edited transcription in collection Readable Joseph Peterson digital edition present.. A practical heptarchical ritual text derived from Dee's angelic records, focused on communicating with planetary or royal angelic powers and employing their offices. It turns diary revelations into grimoire-like working material: prayers, invocations, names, offices, and instructions for dealing with angelic governors. Mysteriorum Libri / Five Books of Mystery: Primus, Secundus, Tertius, Quartus, Quintus

Mysteriorum Libri / Five Books of Mystery: Primus, Secundus, Tertius, Quartus, Quintus is part of Works. John Dee records, with Edward Kelley and other scryers; later transcriptions/editions Multiple readable overlapping editions and individual book PDFs are present.. This is the core early angelic diary sequence, recording Dee's prayers, ritual sessions, angelic communications, tables, names, and instructions from 1581-1583. The material begins from Dee's prayer for wisdom and develops into a working archive of visionary conversations, angelic hierarchies, ritual protocols, and revealed textual/table structures. The individual Primus/Secundus/Tertius/Cuartus/Quintus files and Peterson/Holden-style compilations overlap substantially, so the catalog treats them as one work family with separate file witnesses.
