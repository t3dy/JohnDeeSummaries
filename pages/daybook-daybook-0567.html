Entry text

Being Tuesday, I entered my journey toward Valkenaw glass-house, and Leipzig Mart. There are divers Valkenaws, one by Egre, or Elbogen, not far from Carls Bath, westward from Prague about 18 Bemish miles, another in Behem, about II or 12 miles from Prague, northerly, a mile from Krebitz, and two miles from Leipzig, and a mile from Kamnitz.5
