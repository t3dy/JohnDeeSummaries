Summary

A demonological grimoire framed by a publisher's note and a supposed Dee introduction, then shifting into material attributed to Abd al-Hazred. Its tone, claims, and Lovecraftian elements are strong indicators of pseudepigraphy rather than secure Dee authorship. Useful for reception/occult-fictional Dee, not for Dee's authenticated corpus.
