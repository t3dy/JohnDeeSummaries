Hora n y,. As I sat at dinner with Barthilmew Hickman, my daughter, Patrick, and Thomas Turner, about the end of the dinner Barthilmew heard a voice, saying: 'Tomorrow half an hour after 9 of the clock, give your attendance to know the Lord's pleasure.'
