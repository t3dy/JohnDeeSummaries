Summary

The unprinted works show the scale of Dee's ambitions: British imperial claims, natural philosophy, navigation, historical collections, calendar and mathematical work, and other projects. This subsection is valuable for reconstructing Dee's intellectual self-image and lost or incomplete oeuvre.
