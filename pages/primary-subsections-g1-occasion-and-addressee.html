Summary

The apologetical letter is addressed to ecclesiastical authority and responds to accusations against Dee's reputation. It is a public self-defense, designed to counter the charge that his studies are conjuring or unlawful magic. The tone is defensive but also proud: Dee presents himself as a learned servant of the realm.
