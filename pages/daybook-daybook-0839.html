Records court patronage, household family: My children at this Lady Day in Lent began to go to school at Mordake with the schoolmaster Mr Lee. I gave him his house-rent, and 40S yearly for my three sons and my daughter. The house-rent was almost 41i yearly of Mr Fisher his new house.
