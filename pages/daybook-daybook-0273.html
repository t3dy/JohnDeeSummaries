Entry text

Arthur Dee and Mary Herbert, they being but three year old the eldest, did make as it were a show of childish marriage, of calling each other husband and wife. I note this because of 2t. coming to the cusp of --for seven hours, and 2t. and the }) being in d there the other day. The first day Mary Herbert came to her father's house: and the second day she came to her father's house at East Sheen in Mortlake.1O
