1581-1583 - Conversations with spirits

Index Card

Orientation to the early actions - Primary Sections. Editorial introductions frame Dee's spirit actions as a documentary corpus: devotional prayer, learned natural philosophy, scrying practice, political hope, and ritual experimentation.

Summary

Editorial introductions frame Dee's spirit actions as a documentary corpus: devotional prayer, learned natural philosophy, scrying practice, political hope, and ritual experimentation. They establish the main people and tools: Dee, Edward Kelley/Talbot, earlier scryer Barnabas Saul, the shew-stone, holy table, wax seals, prayers, and the expectation that angelic instruction could recover lost wisdom.

Date scope

1581-1583

Reading notes

Use this as context, not as primary Dee prose. It is useful for chronology, manuscript provenance, and identifying overlap among editions.
