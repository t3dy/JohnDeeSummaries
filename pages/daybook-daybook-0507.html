Entry text

Tuesday, hora 9 fere. I sent the letter to Lord Octavius Spinola through Emericus Sontag, secretary of the Lord Palatine of Siradia. Emericus handed over that letter to the Noble Lord Spinola, immediately after his Imperial Majesty's dinner; and the next day, instead of a reply, he ordered me to come.
