Entry text

By the providence of God, and Mr Gilbert his means, and pacifying of E.K. his vehement passions and pangs, he came again to my house: and my wife very willing, and quieted in mind, and very friendly to E.K. in word and countenance: and a new pacification on all parts confirmed: and all upon the confidence of God his service, to be faithfully and cheerfully intended, and followed in and by our Actions, through the grace and mercy of the Highest.
