E15. The "Communion Of Wives" Crisis - Subsections. The most infamous crisis concerns angelic commands involving the sharing of wives.

Summary

The most infamous crisis concerns angelic commands involving the sharing of wives. The episode is central because it exposes the ethical and social breaking point of the angelic project. Dee records the command, distress, prayer, negotiation, and compliance/aftermath with painful seriousness. It should not be summarized merely as scandal; it is a test case for authority, revelation, marriage, obedience, and Kelley's volatility.
