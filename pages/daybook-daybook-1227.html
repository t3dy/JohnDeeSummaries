Entry text

I paid to John Norton, stationer, 10 pounds in hand: and was bound in a recognisance before Dr Hone for the payment of the rest; IOli yearly: at Christmas and Midsummer 51i, till 53li more 145 8d were paid. Received 30li in part of payment of one hundred for my house at Mortlake ofMr Paget.
