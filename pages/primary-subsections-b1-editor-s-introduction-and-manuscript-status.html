Summary

The editor presents De Heptarchia Mystica as a transcription from Dee's own manuscript, Sloane 3191, and explains its relation to the larger angel diaries. The text is not a raw chronological diary. It is a compiled working manual that extracts and orders material revealed during the actions. The introduction is important because it defines the work as grimoire-like in form but grounded in Dee's records.
