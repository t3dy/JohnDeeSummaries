Entry text

Monday. I made the Rector privy of the injury I endured, and he courteously sent two Masters of Art with me to the Proconsul to have citations for the landlords to appear on Tuesday by 7 of the clock, to answer to our complaint. This Monday night came the Lord Laski from Lasko, upon a letter he had received from me from Nysa, of my coming.
