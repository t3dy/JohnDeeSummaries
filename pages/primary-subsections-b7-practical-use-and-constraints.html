Summary

The manual implies that angelic aid can be sought for knowledge, protection, governance, and practical needs, but only under proper conditions. The text repeatedly binds action to purity, obedience, correct names, correct timing, and divine permission. Its value for LLM work is as a structured index of the ritual system behind the diary conversations. Source witnesses: - `John Dee Sigillum Dei.pdf` - `S D A.pdf` - `SDA.pdf`
