Entry text

Friday: at dinner time, Mr Clerkson and Mr Talbot declared a great deal of Barnabas' naughty dealing toward me: as in telling Mr Clerkson ill things of me that I should mock his friend: and that he was weary of me: that I would so flatter his friend the learned man that I would bereave him of him. But his friend told me (before my wife and Mr Clerkson) that a spiritual creature told him that Barnabas had cosened both Mr Clerkson and me &c. The injuries which this Barnabas had done me divers ways were very great &c.
