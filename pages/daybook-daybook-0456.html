Entry text

Wednesday. We rid to Trittau, four mile from Hamburg, a little village, having left my Lord behind: and also missing my children and servants, which were gone before us another way, to my great grief: till by midnight, by sending out messengers to listen and enquire after them, I heard of them. I, my wife, Rowland, Nurse, and Myrcopskie, my Lord his man.
