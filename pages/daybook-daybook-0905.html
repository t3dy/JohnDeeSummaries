Entry text

Mr Candish received from the Queen's Majesty warrant by word of mouth to assure me to do what I would in philosophy and alchemy, and none should check, control, or molest me; and she said that she would ere long send me 50li more to make up the hundred pound. &c. I gave Mr Can dish the Bishop of Scotland his conclusion, with Marchant. 0 ~. Mr Candish went from Mortlake at 4 of the clock at night toward London and so into Suffolk.
