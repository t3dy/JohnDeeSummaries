Mysteriorum Liber Secundus

Mar. 1582 - Conversations with spirits

Index Card

Mysteriorum Liber Secundus - Primary Sections. The second book deepens the apparatus of the angelic work.

Summary

The second book deepens the apparatus of the angelic work. Dee receives instructions around the seal, holy names, and ritual furniture, with repeated attention to purity, obedience, and the danger of misunderstanding revealed forms. Michael's authority becomes central, and the conversations move from exploratory scrying into a more formal program of preparation.

Date scope

Mar. 1582

Corpus area

Conversations with spirits

Reading notes

Best read beside the Sigillum Dei material, since many later ritual diagrams depend on revelations in this phase.
