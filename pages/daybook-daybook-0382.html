Thursday. After my coming home from the Court, about 4 of the clock after noon, and after being in my study a while, it came into my head to assay to decipher the cipher which before is spoken of, and was brought me by E.K. as he was willed to do.3 And at the first I was half out of all hope: but yet making many assays, and guessing at it (at the length) to be Latin, I found this to be the true alphabet: God giving me the perseverance.
