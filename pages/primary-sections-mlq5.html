Liber Mysteriorum Quintus

Mar.-May 1583 - Conversations with spirits

Index Card

Liber Mysteriorum Quintus is part of Primary Sections. Mar.-May 1583 - Conversations with spirits. The fifth book moves toward culmination of the first phase: fuller angelic hierarchies, more elaborate tables and names, and the sense that Dee's actions are part of a larger providential disclosure. The material connects spiritual instruction to future reform, hidden knowledge, and the proper use of revealed names. It also shows growing dependence on Kelley's visions and the strain of turning visionary events into stable textual records. Source: quintus.pdf, John Dee Mysteriorum Libri Quinque.pdf, John Dees Five Books of Myster Joseph H Peterson.pdf.
