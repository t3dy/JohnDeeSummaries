A True & Faithful Relation: Casaubon preface and framing

Published 1659; framing Dee records - Conversations with spirits

Index Card

A True & Faithful Relation: Casaubon preface and framing is part of Primary Sections. Published 1659; framing Dee records - Conversations with spirits. Casaubon frames Dee's diaries as a cautionary publication about spirits, credulity, demonic deception, and the risks of unlawful curiosity. His preface is not Dee's voice, but it strongly shapes the printed reception of the diaries. It argues over whether the events should be believed, how they relate to possession and revelation, and why publishing them might serve religious warning. Source: John Dee_ Meric Casaubon True faithful relation of what passed for many years between Dr John Dee and some spirits Kessinger Pub.pdf, John Dee True and Faithful Relation (excerpts) - intro by C.pdf.
