Entry text

Saturday. Die Sancti Micha::lis. Remember that this day (after noon) I sent Hugh on foot with my letters to Cracovia to Edmond, that my folk should not be out of quiet or afeared to see Mistress Kelly sent for and no letters to come from me, &c. I writ to the Lord Laski of our want of money, &c.
