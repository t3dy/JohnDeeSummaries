Summary

The epilogue asks that Dee be judged by his writings, services, and known piety rather than rumor. It also carries a note of exhaustion and grievance. For LLM work, use this section whenever evaluating Dee's self-understanding, not merely hostile or later accounts of him. 1. Start with `A Letter Containing...` to understand Dee's public self-defense. 2. Read the Day Books sections F2-F6 for the Mortlake context before and during the first actions. 3. Read Mysteriorum Primus through Quintus in order, using A2-A11 above. 4. Read De Heptarchia Mystica after Mysteriorum Secundus-Quintus, because it organizes revealed material into a working manual. 5. Read the Sigillum witnesses with the PDF images open, not only Markdown extraction. 6. Read TFR after the Five Books for the continental and later actions. 7. Read Liber 84 beside the TFR sections on calls, tables, airs, and governors. 8. Return to the Day Books F10-F21 to cross-check the mundane chronology against the spirit diary chronology.
