Summary

These small files preserve the textual/numerical contents of the Sigillum Dei Aemeth, the holy seal central to Dee's angelic apparatus. They are not prose summaries but diagrammatic data: divine names, letter strings, numerical placements, and manuscript reconstruction notes. Best used as reference material alongside Heptarchia and the angel diaries.
