This section marks the arrival and early significance of Edward Kelley/Talbot. Dee's diary entries show both attraction and risk: Kelley seems to offer the visionary ability Dee needs, but his identity and conduct are unstable. The journal context helps keep the spirit actions tied to household and social reality.
