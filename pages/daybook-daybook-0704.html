Entry text

At dinner time Mr Ed. K. sent his brother Mr Th. K. to me with these words: 'My brother saith that you study too much: and therefore seeing it is too late to go today to Crumlaw, he wi sheth you to come to pass the time with him, at play, &c.' I went after dinner and played, he and I against Mr F. Gar. and Mr Rob. till supper time, in his dining Stove, and after supper he came, and the others, and we played there two or three

K. to me with these words: 'My brother saith that you study too much: and therefore seeing it is too late to go today to Crumlaw, he wi sheth you to come to pass the time with him, at play, &c.'
