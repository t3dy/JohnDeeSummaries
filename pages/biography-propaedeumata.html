Essay

Dee's Propaedeumata aphoristica presented aphorisms on celestial influence, rays, and natural causation.

The book is central to seeing Dee's astrology and optics as natural philosophy rather than as a detached occult curiosity.

Bibliographical reference: Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 98-102; György E. Szőnyi, John Dee's Occultism: Magical Exaltation through Powerful Signs (SUNY Press), pp. 155-181; Håkan Håkansson, Seeing the Word: John Dee and Renaissance Occultism (Lund University), pp. 274-289
