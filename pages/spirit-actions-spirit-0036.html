Action text

∆: As we wer talking of the Macedonian (the grecian), who yesterday cam with Mr. Sanford his letters, there appeared in the corner of my study a blak shadow: and I did charge that shaddow to declare who he was: There cam a voyce and sayd that it was the Macedonian: and abowt his hat was written in great letters this word,, which EK wrote out and it signifieth maculosus, 1352 or condemnatus1353 &c. and the Voyce sayd, that word was sufficient adding Est,1354 ∆: God be thanked and praysed. To me deliuered by Mr. Edward Kelly
