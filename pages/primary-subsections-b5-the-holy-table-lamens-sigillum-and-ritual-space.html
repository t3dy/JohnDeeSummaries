B5. The Holy Table, Lamens, Sigillum, And Ritual Space - Subsections. The ritual furniture is described or invoked as necessary to the heptarchical work.

Summary

The ritual furniture is described or invoked as necessary to the heptarchical work. The holy table, wax seals, golden lamen, and Sigillum Dei Aemeth belong to a consecrated environment. This subsection connects De Heptarchia directly to Mysteriorum Secundus and Quartus. The objects are both material and symbolic mediators.
