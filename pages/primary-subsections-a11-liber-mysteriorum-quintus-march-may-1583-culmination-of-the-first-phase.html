A11. Liber Mysteriorum Quintus: March-May 1583 Culmination Of The First Phase

Early Angel Conversations, 1581-1583

Index Card

A11. Liber Mysteriorum Quintus: March-May 1583 Culmination Of The First Phase is part of Subsections. Early Angel Conversations, 1581-1583. The fifth book gathers the early work into a more mature system: angelic hierarchies, offices, tables, names, future revelations, and Dee's providential role. It prepares the transition to the later continental actions recorded in Casaubon. Thematically, this is where the first phase becomes recognizably "Enochian" in later reception, although Dee's own categories are broader: angelic language, divine books, holy names, and the restoration of wisdom.
