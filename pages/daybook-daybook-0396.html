Entry text

Monday, I went to London, and E.K. remained attending the accomplishing of the promise for the Table's ending and for the perfect form of the holy letters receiving: which two points (when I came home that afternoon) I found done. But it is to be noted that when E.K. could not aptly imitate the form of the characters or letters as they were showed, that then they appeared drawn on his paper with a light yellow colour, which he drew the black upon, and so the yellow colour disappearing there remained only the shape of the letter in black, after this manner and just of this quantity and proportion: CC) ~~1..~~ J,,/ \f1.p c b r
