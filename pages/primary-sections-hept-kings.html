De Heptarchia Mystica: kings, princes, ministers, and offices - Primary Sections. The central section organizes the sevenfold angelic hierarchy: kings, princes, and ministers with specific names, powers, and offices.

Summary

The central section organizes the sevenfold angelic hierarchy: kings, princes, and ministers with specific names, powers, and offices. It translates visionary instruction into a functional system for petitions, invocations, and requests for knowledge or assistance. The recurring pattern is office, name, dignity, and constrained use under God.

Reading notes

Useful for extracting named entities and ritual roles. It should be cross-referenced with the diary sessions that disclose each figure.
