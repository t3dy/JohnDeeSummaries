Entry text

Friday, a meridie. Praga!. Being willed to deal with Rudolphus, first by letter and then by speech, 1 thought good to send the letter before written for the Emperor, by the Noble Don Wilhelmo de St Clemente, the King of Spain his pra!fectus negotiorum with this Emperor. And thereupon by Emericus Sontag, the Lord Laski his secretary, 1 wrote to the aforesaid Don Clemente, and sent it this Bartholomew Day.
