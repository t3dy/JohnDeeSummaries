G2. Schedule Of Printed Works

A Letter Containing A Most Brief Apologetical Discourse

Index Card

G2. Schedule Of Printed Works is part of Subsections. A Letter Containing A Most Brief Apologetical Discourse. Dee lists printed works and known accomplishments to establish public credibility. These include mathematical, philosophical, navigational, and symbolic writings. The point is not merely bibliography; it is evidence that Dee's learning has been open, useful, and respectable.
