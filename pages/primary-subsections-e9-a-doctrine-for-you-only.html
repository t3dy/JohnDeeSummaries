E9. A Doctrine For You Only

A True & Faithful Relation And Later Angelic Actions

Index Card

E9. A Doctrine For You Only is part of Subsections. A True & Faithful Relation And Later Angelic Actions. This phase emphasizes secret doctrine, selective audiences, and the tension between universal reform and restricted revelation. Dee wants the knowledge to benefit Christendom, but the angels repeatedly limit who may hear, when to speak, and how to preserve the mysteries. This creates a tension between prophecy and secrecy.
