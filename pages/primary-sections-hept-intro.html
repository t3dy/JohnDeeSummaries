Summary

The opening of De Heptarchia Mystica explains the authority and purpose of the heptarchical system. Dee frames the material as divinely taught, grounded in the angelic actions, and ordered toward lawful communication with angelic governors. Editorial matter identifies it as a transcription from Dee's manuscript and emphasizes its relation to the larger diaries.

Reading notes
