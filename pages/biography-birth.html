Essay

John Dee was born in London into a family whose Welsh connections and later claims of British antiquity would matter to his self-fashioning.

The event anchors Dee's later attempts to present himself as both a learned European and a counselor with British historical credentials.

Bibliographical reference: Glyn Parry, The Arch-Conjuror of England: John Dee (Yale University Press), pp. 23-27; Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 26-28
