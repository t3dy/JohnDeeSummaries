Entry text

Die Luna:, hora 7 (inchoante) a meridie. After a quarter of an hour (almost) appeared our instructors, as of fore time. NALVAGE: 'There are 30 Calls yet to come. Those 30 are the Calls of 91 princes and spiritual governors, unto whom the earth is delivered as a portion. These bring in and again displace kings and all the governments upon the earth, and vary the natures of things with the variation of every moment. These are generally governed by the twelve angels of the twelve tribes, which are also governed by the seven which stand before the presence of God.B 'In the first air, the ninth, eleventh and seventh angel of the tribes bear rule and govern. Unto the ninth, 7000 and 200 and nine ministering angels are subject. Unto the eleventh 2000, 300, 60. Unto the seventh 5000, 300, 60, two. Count the number.': 'The whole sum of this government amounteth to 14931.' NALVAGE: 'It is right.' E.K.: 'Now Gabriel standeth Up.'9: 'May I be bold to ask you one question? Is the Queen of England alive or dead?' GABRIEL: 'She liveth. I am nothing near the earth.' E.K.: 'The curtain is drawn.' We prayed jointly some prayers. Upon my motion for the Lord Al. Las. how to deal with the Chancellor, the curtain was drawn open. MAPSAMA: 'My name is Mapsama.
