Entry text

We were in great danger of perishing (on the sands called the spits) about midnight. We had (by force of wind contrary) anchored by them, and the anchor came home, no man perceiving it, till the ship was ready to strike on the sands. Then, upon great diligence and policy used by our mariners in hoysing sail and cutting our cable (to leave our shot anchor) and committing ourselves to the hands of God, and most earnestly praying

We were in great danger of perishing (on the sands called the spits) about midnight. We had (by force of wind contrary) anchored by them, and the anchor came home, no man perceiving it, till the ship was ready to strike on the sands.
