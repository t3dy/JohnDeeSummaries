A8. Mysteriorum Liber Secundus: Seal, Names, And Ritual Furniture - Subsections. Liber Secundus deepens the formal apparatus of the work.

Summary

Liber Secundus deepens the formal apparatus of the work. The conversations turn toward holy names, the Sigillum Dei Aemeth, the holy table, wax seals, and the objects needed for lawful action. The spirits' instructions make the material apparatus inseparable from theology: instruments are holy because they are ordered to God, not because they mechanically compel spirits. This section should be read with the one-page Sigillum witnesses and De Heptarchia Mystica.
