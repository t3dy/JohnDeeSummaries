Entry text

Katharin Dee was sent to Petersham, with Goodwife Garret to be nursed of her. Katharin Dee was shifted to Nurse Garret at Petersham, on Friday, the next day after St Lawrence Day: being the lIth day of this month. My wife went on foot with her, and Helen Cole my maid, George and Benjamin in very great showers of rain. I2 Aug. I received letters from Doctor Andrew Hels, learned in occult philosophy &c., through the care in my business of my friend Richard Hesketh, agent of Antwerp, and I received along with my letters the ii mensitam or sigillum planetarum.32
