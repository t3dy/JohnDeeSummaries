Wednesday, mane hora 11K Lubeck. E.K.: 'There is one come in like a ghost. Now all the sides of the Stone are darkish. Now is one come in very brave, like a preacher. I take him to be an evil one.' This preacher-like creature sayeth: 'Are you so foolish to think that the power of God will descend into so base a place, as this corruptible Stone is?' d: 'Wh'o causeth thee to come here?'
