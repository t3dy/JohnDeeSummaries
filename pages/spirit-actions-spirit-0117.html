lodged in the Suburbs by the Church at....... Where we remained a sevenight, and then (I and my wife) we removed to the house in St. Stephens street, which I had hired for a year, for 80 gylders (of 30 gr.) And Master Edward Kelly came to us on Fryday in the Easter week (by the new Gregorian Kalendar) being the 27 day of March, by the old Kalendar: but the sixth day of April, by the new Kalendar, Easter day being the first day of April in Poland, by the new Gregorian institution. CRACOVIA. Martii 13. An. 1584. Omnis Honor, Laus, Gloria & Gratiarum Actio, sit Deo Nostro Omnipotenti, Trino & uni nunc & semper. Amen. D. [1] D. All this while we had kneeled. [2] Vide infra. [3] D. Forte, staggering. [4] E.K., A.L., D. [5] Thou shalt be hanged, he said to E.K. Declina, à malo & Fac bonum. Psalm. [6] Of A.L. and our separating hereafter. [7] An Angel now taking place. [8] * Note, he spake this, pointing down to the great Bible of the Lord Alb. Laskie, upon which the Shew-stone, now, stood. [9] A Prophesie. an. 1587. in Januar 10. Antichristus.. Si simpliciter, sit annas est intelligendus, si non septenarius unus, nostrorum annorum 24 sit habendus pro uno anno mystico: vel 42. menses, &c. [10] D. They are in Paradise; they were carried upward, especially Elias. [11] This sentence is revoked afterward, and the stone dignified. [12] To E.K. [13] D. Infra, in Libre Cracoviensi, Aprilis 21. dixit hanc linguam nunquam fuisse revelatam. Let these two places be reconciled. [14] D. Folio precedente. [15] E.K. He speaketh a language to me unknown, and looketh somewhat on one side. [16] Cabalistical in respect of the receiving. [17] D. Note, at Prague, Aug. 24. I understood that Sir Henry Sidney was not dead in February nor March, no nor in May last: Therefore this must be considered. Doctor Haget his son told me. You may also mark how the Devil at this time did mis-inform E.K. and so it is possible that this being a lye, was his. [18] The red powder. -JHP [19] i.e. The Book of St. Dunstan. -JHP [20] A.L. [21] D. Cacodæmon ipsius Palatinæ. [22] Note. Madimi as a tall woman now. [23] D. I was sick of an ague, and thereupon did somewhat d???, awaiting also for the Lord, A.L. His help, chiefly with Coach, House, and Money. [24] D. Vide Job. cap. 33.
