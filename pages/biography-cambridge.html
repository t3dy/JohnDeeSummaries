1542: Studies at St John's College, Cambridge

1542: Studies at St John's College, Cambridge - Biography. Dee entered Cambridge as a young scholar and was trained in the logical, mathematical, and humanist disciplines that underwrote his later reputation.

Summary

Essay

Dee entered Cambridge as a young scholar and was trained in the logical, mathematical, and humanist disciplines that underwrote his later reputation.

Cambridge gave Dee the institutional beginning for a career that would join mathematics, natural philosophy, instruments, and courtly utility.

Bibliographical reference: Glyn Parry, The Arch-Conjuror of England: John Dee (Yale University Press), pp. 29-35; Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 26-30
