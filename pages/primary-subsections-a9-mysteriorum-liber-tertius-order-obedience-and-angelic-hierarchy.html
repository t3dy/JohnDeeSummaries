Summary

Liber Tertius continues to organize the communications around rank, office, names, and procedure. Dee presses for knowledge and practical certainty, while angelic voices repeatedly insist on humility, timing, preparation, and obedience. The section's pattern is pedagogical: Dee is being schooled into a method rather than simply given information.
