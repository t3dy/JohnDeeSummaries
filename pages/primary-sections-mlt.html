Summary

The third book continues the establishment of angelic authority and ceremonial order. Dee presses for knowledge, protection, and clarity, while the spirits redirect him toward patience, humility, and the correct sequence of divine instruction. The sessions refine names, offices, and procedures rather than offering a single linear doctrine.
