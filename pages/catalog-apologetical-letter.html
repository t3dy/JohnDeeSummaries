Summary

A compact self-defense in which Dee frames his studies, services, and reputation before hostile charges of conjuring or unlawful magic. It is valuable for Dee's public self-presentation: learned service to crown and commonwealth, defense of mathematical and philosophical labor, complaint over poverty or neglect, and insistence that his pursuits are pious and useful rather than diabolical.
