E17. Alchemical Promises And Laboratory Ambitions - Subsections. The later records intertwine angelic revelation with alchemical hopes.

Summary

The later records intertwine angelic revelation with alchemical hopes. Kelley becomes associated with transmutation and courtly alchemical patronage, while Dee continues to frame alchemy within divine restoration. The reader should distinguish Kelley's practical alchemical career from Dee's theological-natural-philosophical hopes.
