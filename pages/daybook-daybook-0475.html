Entry text

Saturday. We passed the dangerous way of ice, having 25 men to cut the ice for our coaches to come through above two English miles long: but for all that great help, we could not get to Vinew City that night, by reason of the great water and ice hard by the town, which was broken over the banks into the meadows very deep.

Saturday.
