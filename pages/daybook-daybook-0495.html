Entry text

Remember that on Saturday, the 14 day of July by the Gregorian calendar, and the 4 day ofJuly by the old calendar, Rowland my child (who was born anno 1583, January 28, by the old calendar) was extremely sick about noon, or midday, and by I of the clock ready to give up the ghost, or rather lay for dead, and his eyes set and sunk into his head, &c. I made a vow, if the Lord did foresee him to be his true servant, and so would grant him life, and confirm him his health itt this danger, and from this danger, I would (during my life) on Saturdays eat but one meal, &c.
