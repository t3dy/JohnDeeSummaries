1595: Appointed Warden of Manchester College

1595; Office and exile

Index Card

1595: Appointed Warden of Manchester College - Biography. Dee received the wardenship of Manchester College, an office that offered employment but placed him away from the courtly center he had long pursued.

Summary

Dee received the wardenship of Manchester College, an office that offered employment but placed him away from the courtly center he had long pursued.

Essay

Dee received the wardenship of Manchester College, an office that offered employment but placed him away from the courtly center he had long pursued.

The appointment can be read both as provision and containment: a late-career office that did not restore Dee's earlier hopes.

Bibliographical reference: Glyn Parry, The Arch-Conjuror of England: John Dee (Yale University Press), pp. 258-273; Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 251-257
