A3. December 22, 1581: First Surviving Action At Mortlake - Subsections. The first surviving action records Dee with Barnabas Saul as scryer.

Summary

The first surviving action records Dee with Barnabas Saul as scryer. The session introduces the shew-stone as a medium, tests the appearing spirit, and raises the question of whether the figure is angelic, deceptive, or ambiguous. Dee is already interested in practical matters, including hidden treasure and secret knowledge, but the larger pattern is discernment. The section shows Dee's early caution: he asks what authority the spirit has, whether the communication is good, and what preparation is required.
