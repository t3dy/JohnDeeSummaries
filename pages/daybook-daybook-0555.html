Entry text

Niepolomice, at the Court of the King of Poland. A meridie horam circiter 6. The King sent for the Lord Laski and me, by his Vice- Chamberlain, whom we came unto in a chamber, within the chamber or room where he useth to give audience, or to eat with his palatines and other. He sat by the window which is toward the south, and by which his prospect is into his new garden, which is in making.
