Index Card

Liber 84 and the Forty-Eight Keys/Calls is part of Primary Sections. Later angelic corpus, especially 1584 material - Revealed texts and calls. Liber 84 preserves revealed textual/table material connected with the angelic or Enochian language, elemental tablets, seniors, calls, and names. It is less a narrative diary than a structured body of revealed language and magical geography. The section is important for the transition from the early angelic sessions to the later system of calls, watchtowers, governors, and tabular correspondences.
