Entry text

I delivered to Mr Ed. Kelly (earnestly requiring it, as his part) the half of all the ~ Animal which was made; it is to wit 20 ounces: he weighed it himself in my chamber: he brought his weights purposely for it. My Lord had spoken to me before for some: but Mr Kelly had not spoken.
