KELLY ASCENDANT, 1587-1589 - topics: miscellaneous

Index Card

8 Feb. - Daybook Entries. Records a brief diary notice: Mr E.K.

Summary

Records a brief diary notice: Mr E.K. at 9 of the clock after noon sent for me to his laboratory over the gate: to see how he distilled sericon, according as in time past and of late he heard of me out of Riplay, &c. God lead his heart to all charity and brotherly love, &c.
