E10. Audience Of The Emperor - Subsections. Dee's hoped-for imperial audience with Rudolf II brings the angelic project into direct contact with European power.

Summary

Dee's hoped-for imperial audience with Rudolf II brings the angelic project into direct contact with European power. Dee presents himself as bearer of divine warning and reforming knowledge. The section is important for understanding Dee not merely as a private mystic but as a courtly and diplomatic actor.
