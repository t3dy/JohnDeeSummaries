Summary

The later actions continue after the early Mysteriorum sequence. Dee and Kelley leave the Mortlake-centered world and enter a mobile, high-stakes environment involving Laski, Poland, Prague, Trebona, Rudolf II, Catholic authorities, and reformist expectations. The angelic project becomes more public and politically dangerous.
