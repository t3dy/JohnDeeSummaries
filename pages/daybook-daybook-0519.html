Entry text

Monday, a meridie circa 3 horam. Prag:e. Having finished the Lord's Prayer, eYc., we were granted an apparition very quickly. E.K.: 'Here be two. One his face is covered and the other is not: and he whose face is uncovered seemeth to be Gabriel.': 'Our chief cause (at this instant) why we resort to this Show-Stone is for to understand (according to the grounds of God his promise) the state of my wife her grievous disease, and means to cure her, wherein if it please the Lord to be merciful unto her and me, it shall be the occasion whereby she will all her life praise the name of God for his mercies, and be of a quieter mind, and not so testy and fretting as she is.'
