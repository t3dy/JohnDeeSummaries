Summary

An extensive occult manual presented under Dee's name, with material on Rosicrucian secrets, magical practice, and later esoteric transmission. The opening notes and Golden Dawn-era framing make it very different from secure Dee manuscripts. Cataloged here because the collection labels it as Dee, but it should be treated as doubtful or pseudepigraphal unless corroborated separately.
