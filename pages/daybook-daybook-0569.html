Entry text

A decree of exile against us went out on the orders of the Emperor Rudolph. May-July. Be it remembered that Mr Francis Pucci, Florentine, whom at my going to Leipzig I left in our house at Prague, in the mean space had been at Frankfurt upon Main, and went from Prague about the same day that the Bishop of Piacenza, Nuncius Apostolicus, did exhibit to the Emperor a libel against us of most horrible untruths, &c. 8 At his going from Prague he had that Nuncio's blessing, &c. In the mean space, and before his return to Prague, again it happened so, that the 30 of May last I received the Emperor's commandment: I, my companion and all mine to depart within six days out of Prague and so consequently out of all his kingdoms, dukedoms and lands. Which we did, and were come to Erfurt in Thuringia, and sometimes to Kassel9 whither f!,. 196 f!,.
