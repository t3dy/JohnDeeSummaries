Entry text

On Sunday, the stage at Paris Garden fell down, all at once, being full of people beholding the bear-baiting: many being killed thereby, more hurt, and all amazed. eYe. The godly expound it as a due plague of God for the wickedness there used, and the Sabbath day so profanely spent.4
