B3. The Heptarchical Kings And Princes

Index Card

B3. The Heptarchical Kings And Princes - Subsections. The central system organizes seven angelic kings and their associated princes.

Summary

The central system organizes seven angelic kings and their associated princes. Each figure has a dignity, name, office, and domain of action. The sevenfold structure is planetary and royal: the angels govern aspects of earthly and celestial operation under God. The reader should track names, offices, and hierarchy rather than expecting a narrative.
