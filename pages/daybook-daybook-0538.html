Entry text

Monday. Mane, horam circiter 7. Praga:. First we poured out some prayers to God, according to our custom; then (after a pause) we made some other verbal requests for the mercy, enlightenment and help of God, &c. Finally, when no apparition had come after 2}'i hours, we ceased. But I was afraid of the great wrath of God and was reluctant to provoke him, &c. l1: 'Lord have mercy on us, and do not deal with us according to all our iniquities. Amen.'
