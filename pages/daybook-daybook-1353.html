Entry text

Barthilmew went-and John Crocker. John Crocker (my good servant) had leave to go to see his parents. He went with Barthilmew Hickman and Robert Charles toward Braybrooke, with Arthur Golding, to cure of his fistula. John Crocker intendeth to return about Easter or else Whitsuntide next. God be his God speed. Mr Humphry Damport, made our steward. I2 Oct. Hor. 12, Rafe Holden's bill put up at the sessions. He preferred a bill against Richard Walkedine, and Antony Ryve, for Brereriding's chase entering &c, which is false &c. The bill was not found.
