1608 or 1609: Death at Mortlake

1608 or 1609; Afterlife

Index Card

1608 or 1609: Death at Mortlake - Biography. Dee died in poverty and diminished public standing, though his books, manuscripts, objects, and reputation continued to circulate.

Summary

Dee died in poverty and diminished public standing, though his books, manuscripts, objects, and reputation continued to circulate.

Essay

Dee died in poverty and diminished public standing, though his books, manuscripts, objects, and reputation continued to circulate.

His death did not end the historical life of the materials: later editors, antiquaries, alchemists, and scholars remade Dee through reception.

Bibliographical reference: Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 249-267; Glyn Parry, The Arch-Conjuror of England: John Dee (Yale University Press), pp. 273-322
