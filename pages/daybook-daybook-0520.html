Entry text

Tuesday, mane hora 9. Praga:. We finished our prayers to God. E.K.: 'Here they be.' They paused before they spake, very long. d: 'Perhaps they expect our answer, as concerning our cleansing and abstaining from sins. Herein I crave at God's hands his grace and mercy, and intend and desire both to be purged, and also to abstain not only 40 days but all my life, to the best of my ability, &c.'
