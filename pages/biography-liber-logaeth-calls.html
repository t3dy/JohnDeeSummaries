Summary

The angelic project produced revealed language, tables, calls, and names associated with later Enochian materials.

Essay

The angelic project produced revealed language, tables, calls, and names associated with later Enochian materials.

These materials are central to scholarly arguments about Dee's search for a perfect or Adamic language and a true cabala of nature.

Bibliographical reference: Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 199-213; György E. Szőnyi, John Dee's Occultism: Magical Exaltation through Powerful Signs (SUNY Press), pp. 181-241; Håkan Håkansson, Seeing the Word: John Dee and Renaissance Occultism (Lund University), pp. 309-318
