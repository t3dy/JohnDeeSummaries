Records household family, money legal, letters books: I had my copy of Mr Roger Dale our steward, and had 51i, the fine released of the L. his bountifulness. I told the steward that I had bought the house of Mr Mark Perpoint, and he desired to see the note of his copy: and so I did eYe.
