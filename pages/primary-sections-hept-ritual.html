De Heptarchia Mystica: prayers, invocations, table, seal, and practice

Ritual use - Ritual compilation

Index Card

De Heptarchia Mystica: prayers, invocations, table, seal, and practice is part of Primary Sections. Ritual use - Ritual compilation. The practical sections specify prayers, invocatory forms, furnishings, the table, Sigillum Dei, lamens, and procedural constraints. Dee's concern is not merely naming spirits but preparing a lawful devotional environment. The text repeatedly binds technique to piety, divine permission, and correct timing.
