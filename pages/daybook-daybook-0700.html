Entry text

Thomas Simkinson and Mr Francis Garland's brother Robert came to Trebon from England, thinking we were ready to come into England upon the Queen's letters sent for us. One other brother came also and stayed there. The same day: Mr John Carpio invited Mr Kelly and me, &c., to his marriage, after Christmas.
