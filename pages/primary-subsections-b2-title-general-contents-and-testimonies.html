Summary

The opening sections justify the text's authority and summarize its scope. Dee frames the heptarchical material as divinely taught, connected to creation, and useful for lawful communication with angelic powers. The "testimonies" draw on earlier diary utterances to establish that the instructions are not Dee's invention but revealed and confirmed over time.
