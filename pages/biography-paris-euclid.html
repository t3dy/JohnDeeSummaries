1550: Lectures on Euclid in Paris - Biography. Dee's Paris lectures on Euclid helped establish his continental reputation as a mathematical teacher and interpreter.

Summary

Dee's Paris lectures on Euclid helped establish his continental reputation as a mathematical teacher and interpreter.

Essay

Dee's Paris lectures on Euclid helped establish his continental reputation as a mathematical teacher and interpreter.

The event anticipates the later Mathematical Preface and Dee's lifelong claim that mathematics mediates practical, natural, and metaphysical knowledge.

Bibliographical reference: Deborah E. Harkness, John Dee's Conversations with Angels: Cabala, Alchemy, and the End of Nature (Cambridge University Press), pp. 28-30; György E. Szőnyi, John Dee's Occultism: Magical Exaltation through Powerful Signs (SUNY Press), pp. 155-181
