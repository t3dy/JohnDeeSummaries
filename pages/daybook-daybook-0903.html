Entry text

Donum It'. A meridie circa 3 recepi a Regina Domina 50li. 25 in angelottis et 25 in argent. Mr Thomas Griffith my cousin of Llanbeder came to see me. Lay all night with me, and also Mr Thomas Jones, and on the Monday morning went by water to London: and so the same day homeward.
