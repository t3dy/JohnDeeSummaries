Entry text

Monday. Mr John Leonard Haller, of Hallerstein by Worms in Germany, came again to me, to declare his readiness to go toward Quinsay: and how he would go and lie at Venice all this winter, and from thence to Constantinople, &c. I requested Mr Charles Sled to help him to make his money over to Paris and Nuremberg, and to help him with the searcher of Rye to pass his horse: and to help him with Mr Osborn the Alderman with his letters to Constantinople.19
