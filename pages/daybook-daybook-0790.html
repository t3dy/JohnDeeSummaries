Summary

Records court patronage, letters books, angelic spiritual: I gave to Mr Ed. Kelly my glass, so highly and long esteemed of our Queen and the Emperor Rudolph the Second &c, of which mention was made in the preface to Euclid.30 The letter of 500,000 ducats required.
