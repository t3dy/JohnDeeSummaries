Summary

In early Kelley actions, Dee discusses the Book of Soyga/Aldaraia and his desire to read its tables. This is one of the strongest links between Dee's preexisting library-based scholarship and the angelic project. The angels' replies imply that certain books are of angelic or Adamic origin and require angelic interpretation. For LLM analysis, connect this section to Harkness chapter 5: divine language, true cabala, and the Book of Nature.
