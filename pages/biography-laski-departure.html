September 1583: Leaves England with Albert Laski

September 1583: Leaves England with Albert Laski - Biography. Dee, his household, and Kelley left England with the Polish noble Albert Laski, carrying the angelic project into Central Europe.

Summary

Dee, his household, and Kelley left England with the Polish noble Albert Laski, carrying the angelic project into Central Europe.

Essay

Dee, his household, and Kelley left England with the Polish noble Albert Laski, carrying the angelic project into Central Europe.

The departure changed the angelic conversations from a Mortlake household practice into a project entangled with continental patronage and apocalyptic politics.

Bibliographical reference: Glyn Parry, The Arch-Conjuror of England: John Dee (Yale University Press), pp. 183-200; György E. Szőnyi, John Dee's Occultism: Magical Exaltation through Powerful Signs (SUNY Press), pp. 241-281
