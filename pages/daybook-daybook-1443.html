Entry text

Hor. 9Y, ante meridiem. BARTHILMEW: 'The creature.' RAPHAEL: 'John Dee, if thou dost doubt of any thing as concerning the entering into the course of thy journey, now speak. For God will most mercifully and graciously be thy keeper, and deliver thee from the hands of thy enemies in thy journey. And for the good health of thy body, God will so carry thee in good health, that thou shalt set forth such service when thou art there placed, that shall be thy great comfort unto God's honour, in making of his marvellous works to be known. And so I end.'
