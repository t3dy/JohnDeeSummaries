Entry text

Paid this day 19li in Mortlake. Remember that on Passion Sunday, being the 21 of March by old account, all things was paid for to Mr Thomas Hudson, for wood and corn, about 14li. At his house, when he was sick of the strangury. Also to Goodman Bedell was paid 4li for billet, bales, and lose faggot the same day. Likewise to Goodwife Welder 8s for one month's nursing of Madimi: and 4S more beforehand.
