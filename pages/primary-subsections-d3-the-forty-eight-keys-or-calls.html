Index Card

D3. The Forty-Eight Keys Or Calls is part of Subsections. Liber 84 / Liber LXXXIV. The Calls are ritual utterances associated with angelic language and access to specific angelic domains. In this source, they appear as a central revealed linguistic corpus. The subsection should be read as an extension of the divine-language and true-name concerns found in the later conversations.
