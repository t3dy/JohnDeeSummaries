Entry text

My servant Lettice, discharged, and fully paid. She went with Rob. Charles by water, hor. 2J!,. after dinner. I discharged Lettice of my service and paid all duties until this day: her year ending on the 8 of April. I gave her for a month over 2S 6d and for to spend by the way I gave her 2S 6d, Robert Charles and my wife being by in my study.
