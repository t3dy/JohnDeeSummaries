Entry text

Remember that on Saturday afternoon, the Chancellor came to Cracow with 60 coaches in his company and train: he bringing in a close coach (covered with red) the Lord Samuel Sborosky prisoner, whom he took on Thursday/Friday night before, at his sister's house, being separated from his soldiers and servants, &c.7
