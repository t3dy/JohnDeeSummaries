Summary

This travel section follows Dee, Kelley, and their households into continental movement. It records logistics, patronage hopes, spiritual instructions, and the problem of maintaining the angelic work while displaced. The angelic conversations are now entangled with travel, dependency on aristocratic patrons, and the uncertain reception of Dee's prophetic claims.
