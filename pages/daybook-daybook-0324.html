Entry text

She vomited this Monday morning very much green stuff and of to 45 to the stool likewise. A meridie hor. 3Yz came Sir George Peckham to me, to know the title for Norombega: in respect of Spain and Portugal parting the whole world's discoveries. He promised me of his gift out of his patent 5000 acres of the new conquest, and thought to get so much of Mr Gerard's gift, to be sent me with seal, within a few days. 14 Mr William Pole, whom Phillip Simons, sometimes barber to the old Earl ofTavistock, doth know, came to me: and made acquaintance with me: promised to come again within 14 days. Jane my wife went to Nurse Garret's to pay her 125 for her wages due till Friday last, which was St Margaret's Day: and brought her I2d for candles.
