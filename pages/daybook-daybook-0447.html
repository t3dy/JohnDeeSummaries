Entry text

Thursday. We came from Anjum to Emden,5 going without the Isles, and coming in at Wester Emb. We came before the town by 6 of the clock after noon, but could not get in at the gates, and therefore we lay all night a-shipboard. But my Lord A.L. went over to the lodging on the other shore.
