ADIEU TO THE COURT AND COURTING, 1595-1601

Topics

travel

Entry text

Be it remembered that Sir George Booth came to Manchester to visit Mr Humphry Damport, counsellor of Gray's Inn: and so came to the College to me: and after a few words of discourse, we agreed as concerning our two tenements in Dunham Massey in his occupying &c. That he and I with the Fellows would stand to the arbitrement of the said Mr Damport, after his next return hither from London. John Ratclyf, Mr Damport's man, was with him here, and Mr Dumbell, but they heard not our agreement. We were in my dining-room.
