Entry text

Mr Clerkson and his friend came to my house. Barnabas went home again about 3 of the clock, he lay not at my house now; he went I say on Thursday, when Mr Clerkson came. The sky seemed to be on fire, and to grow red like blood as it spread in different directions, at about 9 at night, especially to the north and east: while overhead, the blood-red clouds were carried toward the south. 12
