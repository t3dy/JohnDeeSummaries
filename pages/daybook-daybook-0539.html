Entry text

Wednesday, mane. Note-E.K. yesterday had a show of a little thing as big as a pease of fire as it were in the Stone going about by the brinks. And because it was not in shape humane, he of purpose would not declare it so to me, and so 1 have noted (as appeareth) of no show. This he told me on Tuesday night (that was yesternight) upon occasion of a great stir and moving in his brains, very sensible and distinct, as of a creature of humane shape and lineaments going up and down to and fro in his brains and within his skull: sometimes seeming to sit down, sometime to put his head out at his ear. 6
