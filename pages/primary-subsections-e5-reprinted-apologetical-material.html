E5. Reprinted Apologetical Material

A True & Faithful Relation And Later Angelic Actions

Index Card

E5. Reprinted Apologetical Material is part of Subsections. A True & Faithful Relation And Later Angelic Actions. Casaubon's volume includes Dee's own apologetic material to show the reader Dee's self-defense against charges of conjuring. This creates a layered text: Casaubon's anti-delusion frame surrounds Dee's own insistence that his studies are godly, useful, and misrepresented.
