Summary

This is the core early angelic diary sequence, recording Dee's prayers, ritual sessions, angelic communications, tables, names, and instructions from 1581-1583. The material begins from Dee's prayer for wisdom and develops into a working archive of visionary conversations, angelic hierarchies, ritual protocols, and revealed textual/table structures. The individual Primus/Secundus/Tertius/Cuartus/Quintus files and Peterson/Holden-style compilations overlap substantially, so the catalog treats them as one work family with separate file witnesses.
