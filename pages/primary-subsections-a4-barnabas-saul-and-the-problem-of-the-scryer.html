A4. Barnabas Saul And The Problem Of The Scryer - Subsections. Saul's role reveals that Dee's angelic work is collaborative from the beginning.

Summary

Saul's role reveals that Dee's angelic work is collaborative from the beginning. Dee depends on another person's visionary capacity, which creates a recurring epistemic problem: Dee writes and interprets what he himself often cannot see. Saul also shows that Dee experimented with scryers before Kelley. The main themes are trust, mediation, household access, and the tension between revelation and possible deception.
