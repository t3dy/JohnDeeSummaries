E18. Later Fragments And 1607 Actions - Subsections. Casaubon includes later fragmentary records, including material from 1607.

Summary

Casaubon includes later fragmentary records, including material from 1607. These are much thinner than the central Dee-Kelley years but important because they show Dee's continuing interest in angelic contact long after the main partnership collapsed. They also shaped Casaubon's sense that Dee persisted in error.
