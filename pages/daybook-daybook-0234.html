Entry text

At the first day of September it did no more ascend, as it did the first day of setting it into balneum: although it had a very smart heat of the balneum in the end: and therefore Mr Harry Waters took it out and set it in the cupboard, ready for to make connection. But the potter had been too slack with the furnace's baking.
