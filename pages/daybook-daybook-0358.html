Entry text

The right honourable Mr Secretary Walsingham came to my house: where by good hap he found Mr Adrian Gilbert, and so talk was begun of north-west straits discovery. eYe. The Bishop of St David's (Mar. Middleton) came to visit me with Mr John Herbert, eYe. The Lord Grey came to Mr Secretary, and so they went together.
