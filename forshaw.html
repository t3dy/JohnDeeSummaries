TitleThe Early Alchemical Reception of John Dee's Monas Hieroglyphica
SectionThe Early Alchemical Reception of John Dee's Monas Hieroglyphica

Forshaw surveys late sixteenth- and seventeenth-century Latin, French, and German alchemical receptions of Dee's Monas, showing its use in cabalistic calculation, symbolic number, and chemical notation.
