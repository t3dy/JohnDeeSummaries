SectionPythagorean Number Symbolism, Alchemy, and the Disciplina Noua

Clucas argues that Dee's Monas draws deeply on Pythagorean arithmology, especially Trithemian number symbolism, and that Dee imagined it as a disciplina noua broader than alchemy alone.

TitleThe Royal Typographer and the Alchemist: John Dee, Willem Silvius, and the Diagrammatic Alchemy of the Monas Hieroglyphica
SectionThe Royal Typographer and the Alchemist

Clucas examines Dee, Willem Silvius, and the diagrammatic/typographical presentation of the Monas to show how alchemical meaning was made through printed form and visual arrangement.
