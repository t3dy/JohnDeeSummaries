Politics in the Margins: The Careers of John Dee

Politics in the Margins: The Careers of John Dee is part of Scholarship. William H. Sherman; chapter 1; key terms: career; patronage; marginality; counsel; Elizabethan politics; learned service. Politics in the Margins: The Careers of John Dee is a chapter in John Dee: The Politics of Reading and Writing in the English Renaissance. Sherman introduces Dee by making marginality itself political: Dee's career is reconstructed through failed offices, patronage negotiations, manuscript traces, and learned self-fashioning rather than through a simple rise-and-fall biography. Its scholarly importance is that it replaces the isolated magus with a reader-writer operating inside Elizabethan systems of access, dependence, counsel, and reputation.
